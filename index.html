What we do

Research designed for your business.

Clients rarely arrive with a research brief. They arrive with a business question. We start with that question and its constraints: budgets and timelines are real, and we design the study to them. We maintain strong partnerships with sample providers, programmers, and statisticians to ensure high security and quality standards. We believe in soft-launches, verifying the data, and multi-day fielding to make sure we are reaching real people, not bots or professional respondents. The learnings come back in the form the team needs: an executive presentation, a detailed report, or interactive tools the team continues to use.

AI Discovery
AI Discovery

Before AI can elevate your business, build the foundation.

AI Discovery is the newest use of our oldest skill. Most organizations know they should be using AI but have no credible way to choose where or how, because the hard part was never the technology. It is discovery. The knowledge AI needs to run a business lives in people's heads, in the brand promise, and in how the work really gets done. Encoding it starts with drawing it out faithfully, and that is the work of research. Together we build the foundational work necessary for AI to elevate. We have already done this inside our own practice, on processes that change only the speed and cost of the research, never its rigor, and the same approach works in any business.

The companies that win with AI will not have the best models. They will have the best-organized understanding of their own work – and that is research.

The inside lens · the work

Map the workflows

Every engagement starts by taking stock: what has been tried with AI, and which parts of the business make the most sense to explore first. We map the workflows, tools, and knowledge with the team, learning where the time, friction, and rework go, and which tasks employees would be glad to automate, flagging the data each task needs so any build starts with known requirements. We weigh each opportunity on impact, feasibility, and human fit, and decide together what to prioritize. Automation only counts as a win when the reclaimed hours move to judgment, relationships, and creativity.

The outside lens · the customer

Tested against the brand promise

Customers judge a brand in seconds, on feeling, before any reasoning happens. When someone wants a human and gets a phone tree or a chatbot, the brand pays for it at that gut level, and no argument about efficiency wins them back. So every automation that touches the customer is weighed on brand fit and tested against the brand promise before it gets built. The result is an executive-ready roadmap: what each option requires, how we will define what good looks like, and how to sequence it. When you're ready, we help you pilot one and measure the real before-and-after.

Hours saved at the brand's expense are borrowed, not earned.
